## Scope and method

**Question sets analyzed (3):** 2076, 2078, 2079.

**Syllabus coverage:** Units 1–9 (full syllabus).

**How “frequency” is counted:**

- `x/3` means the topic appears in `x` out of the 3 analyzed sets (at least once in that set).

**Exam pattern seen in the sets:**

- **Section A:** Attempt any 2 out of 3
- **Section B:** Attempt any 8 out of 12

**Important limitation:** only 3 old sets are available here, so frequency values should be treated as directional (still useful for prioritization).

Priority tags used below:

- **P1 (Core):** repeats strongly and is reliable for scoring
- **P2 (High):** repeats and is scoring
- **P3 (Support):** appears less often in the available sets
